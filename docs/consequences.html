DIAGNOSELAB / REPAIR CONSEQUENCES

A consistent repair.
But what follows from it?

A query can follow from every repair, from only some repairs, or from none. Being true in one surviving model is a different question. Change the repair policy and watch the answer change.

Exact tiny propositional logic. All minimal, minimum-cost and minimum-cardinality repair families retain every tie. Inspect complete models, countermodels and DPLL checks of each repaired formula with the query negated. No probability, real diagnosis or certified proof claim.

"Entails" means true in all models of one repaired formula. "Possible" means true in some model of that formula. Some repair entailing the query is stronger than some repair merely permitting it. For an empty repair family all four answers are null/inapplicable, never vacuous certainty.

04 / WHAT THESE ANSWERS MEAN
Logical certainty is conditional.
Every answer depends on the supplied hard clauses, soft clauses, costs and chosen repair family. A minimum-cost policy may reverse the answer from a minimum-cardinality policy. There is no single best policy here. Counts of models are not probabilities. A countermodel disproves a logical consequence; it does not establish a real-world situation is possible or safe.
All 2^m soft subsets and 2^n complete assignments are enumerated.
